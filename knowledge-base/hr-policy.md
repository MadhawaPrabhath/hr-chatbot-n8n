# Lanka Tech Solutions (Pvt) Ltd – Employee HR Policy (Sample)
Version 1.0 – Effective 1 January 2026. This is a fictional policy for demo purposes.

## 1. Working Hours
Normal working hours are 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Employees may start between 7:30 AM and 9:30 AM (flexi-time) but must complete 8 working hours per day.
Work on Saturdays is only required when approved by the department head.

## 2. Work From Home (WFH)
Employees who have completed probation may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval.
WFH requests must be submitted on the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance.

## 3. Annual Leave
Permanent employees receive 14 days of annual leave per calendar year.
In the first year of employment, annual leave is calculated pro-rata based on the date of joining.
Annual leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.

## 4. Casual Leave
Employees receive 7 days of casual leave per year for personal matters.
Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be combined with annual leave without manager approval.

## 5. Sick Leave
Employees receive 7 days of paid sick leave per year.
For sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be submitted to HR within 3 days of returning.

## 6. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 84 working days of paid maternity leave.
Male employees are entitled to 3 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within one month of the child's birth.

## 7. Probation
New employees are on probation for 6 months.
During probation, employees are not eligible for WFH, and the notice period is 2 weeks.

## 8. Salary and Payroll
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th is a holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day.
Payslips are available on the HR portal.
The company contributes 12% to EPF and 3% to ETF. Employees contribute 8% to EPF.

## 9. Medical Insurance
Permanent employees are covered by the company medical insurance scheme up to LKR 150,000 per year for hospitalisation.
OPD claims up to LKR 25,000 per year can be submitted with original bills within 30 days.

## 10. Dress Code
Smart casual is the standard dress code from Monday to Thursday.
Fridays are casual dress days. Formal attire is required for client meetings.

## 11. Resignation and Notice Period
Confirmed employees must give 1 month's written notice of resignation to their manager and HR.
All company assets (laptop, ID card) must be returned on the last working day.

## 12. Contact HR
For questions not covered in this policy, contact the HR team at hr@lankatech.example or extension 210.
HR office hours are 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on weekdays.
